The working sequence

Four stages.
No mystery.

01
DISCOVER

Understand the goal.

We learn about your business, audience, current situation and what needs to improve. You share references and priorities; we ask the questions that shape a useful brief.

  • Project goals and audience
  • Current assets and references
  • Success criteria and constraints
02
DEFINE

Agree the scope.

We outline the deliverables, timeline, investment, communication rhythm and revision terms in writing before production starts.

  • Deliverables and exclusions
  • Milestones and timeline
  • Quotation and payment terms
03
CREATE

Make the work real.

We produce against the agreed direction, share work at the planned review points and use feedback to refine the details that matter.

  • Focused production
  • Review and feedback
  • Agreed revision rounds
04
DELIVER

Hand over with care.

We prepare the final files, links and practical handover notes. Any ongoing support or next phase is discussed separately and clearly.

  • Final export or deployment
  • Organised files and links
  • Next-step recommendations
