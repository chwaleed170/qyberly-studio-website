THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Property businesses often rely on scattered listings, unclear details and enquiry paths that make it harder for a visitor to take the next step. NOVA explores a more guided experience.

THE STRATEGIC RESPONSE

Lead with a strong value proposition, make property categories easy to scan, surface essential listing information and keep viewing enquiries visible throughout the journey.

WHAT THE CONCEPT INCLUDES
  • Responsive homepage and property discovery layout
  • Property cards with location, type and key details
  • Clear viewing enquiry calls to action
  • Mobile-first hierarchy and accessible navigation
  • Search-ready page structure and metadata plan
INTENDED BUSINESS OUTCOME

Turn browsing into a more confident enquiry.

Designed to help prospective buyers understand the offer, compare relevant properties and contact the business with less friction.

SUCCESS METRICS TO TRACK
01Property detail views
02Viewing CTA click-through rate
03Enquiry form completion rate
04Qualified enquiries by source

No live analytics or conversion lift is claimed. These are the measurement points to instrument in a real deployment.

THE BRAND CHALLENGE

In a crowded coffee category, a new brand needs more than a logo. It needs a recognisable point of view that can stay coherent across packaging, social content and everyday customer touchpoints.

THE STRATEGIC RESPONSE

Build ROVE around the idea of coffee as a deliberate daily ritual. A confident wordmark, warm roast-inspired palette and direct copy create a flexible identity with a clear personality.

CAMPAIGN DELIVERABLES
  • Primary wordmark and compact brand mark direction
  • Colour, typography and graphic language
  • Two coffee packaging concepts
  • Social launch post and product-story templates
  • Brand voice and campaign message examples
INTENDED BUSINESS OUTCOME

Make the brand recognisable at every touchpoint.

Give a new coffee label a consistent identity system that supports product recognition, launch communication and repeatable content production.

SUCCESS METRICS TO TRACK
01Branded search and profile visits
02Social saves, shares and profile actions
03Product-page visits from campaign posts
04Repeat purchase and customer recall

This is a visual identity and campaign concept, not a real coffee company or a reported market launch.

THE CAMPAIGN CHALLENGE

Short-form feeds are fast. A product launch needs to stop the scroll, communicate a clear idea quickly and make the next action obvious, even when viewers watch without sound.

THE CREATIVE RESPONSE

Use movement as the visual hook. Build a 30-second hero edit around fast opening cuts, tactile close-ups, purposeful pacing, bold on-screen captions and a clean brand reveal.

PRODUCTION PACKAGE
  • 30-second hero film treatment and edit map
  • Three short vertical cutdowns with distinct hooks
  • Shot list, storyboard and transition notes
  • Caption-first edit with sound-off readability
  • Platform-ready 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 export plan
INTENDED BUSINESS OUTCOME

Turn attention into product consideration.

A reusable launch asset system that introduces the brand, communicates its energy and gives the audience a clear route to explore the collection.

SUCCESS METRICS TO TRACK
01Three-second and hold-rate performance
02Average watch time and completion rate
03Shares, saves and profile visits
04Landing-page sessions and product clicks
