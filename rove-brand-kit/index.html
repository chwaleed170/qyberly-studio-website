05 / APPLICATION NOTES

One system. Many touchpoints.

Use the wordmark as the primary signature. Keep packaging information high-contrast and easy to scan. Social layouts should pair one strong message with one clear product or ritual image. Maintain generous negative space and use terracotta as an accent, not a background for every asset.
